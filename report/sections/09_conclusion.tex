\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This report presented \system, a low-latency moment retrieval pipeline on the \dataset validation split that focuses on reducing the cost of multimodal localization rather than replacing it. The strongest design in this study is a staged dense-shortlist-to-\xml pipeline. At the main top-5 operating point, it improves VR R@1 from 1.69 to 11.56 and VCMR R@1 at IoU 0.5 from 0.38 to 1.43 while keeping SVMR R@1 at IoU 0.5 unchanged at 15.60, and it reduces average validation wall-clock time from 7.36 to 4.96\,ms/query for a 1.48$\times$ speedup over full \xml.

The key trade-off to remember is that dense subtitle retrieval is most valuable as a routing mechanism, not as a replacement for cross-modal temporal grounding. Cheap text retrieval should decide where the expensive model looks, while \xml remains responsible for final localization quality. The most promising next steps are better learned multimodal routing and stronger retrieval encoders that can raise shortlist recall without giving back the latency savings demonstrated here.
